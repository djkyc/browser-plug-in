## Detailed description
Multi Mail Notifier checks all your Gmail, Outlook, Hotmail, Live and Proton Mail accounts in one place and shows unread mail in a single popup — no need to open each inbox.

**What it does**
- Shows unread counts for every account, with a per-account color badge so you can tell them apart at a glance.
- Click an account icon in the left rail to filter that account's mail; click again to show all.
- Read, mark as read, delete, and reply to mail right from the popup.
- One-click copy of login verification codes from mail.
- Optional desktop notifications when new mail arrives.
- Optional automatic update check (every 12 hours) with a one-click download link.
- WebDAV backup and restore of your account list and settings (tokens and passwords are never backed up).

**Sign-in**
- Gmail and Outlook sign in with OAuth (your own Google / Microsoft app). No passwords are stored in the extension.
- Proton Mail reads from a Proton web tab you already signed in to.

**Privacy**
- Your mail is read only to show it to you. Nothing is sent to any third-party server except the mail providers themselves (Google, Microsoft, Proton) and your own WebDAV server if you enable backup.
- See the full privacy policy: https://github.com/djkyc/browser-plug-in/blob/main/multi-mail-notifier/PRIVACY.md
